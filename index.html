Origin

From stream bot to continuity stack.

Bedroom Labs began as an attempt to build a stream chatbot that could be more than static commands and canned replies. The work quickly moved beyond chat behavior into harder questions of memory, continuity, runtime, and persistence. That path became the foundation for the LPE stack.
