Living Persistent Entities

The investment thesis

Bedroom Labs is not just building AI with memory.

Current AI systems can respond, retrieve, and automate — but they do not become unique through lived experience.

Living Persistent Entities are designed around autobiographical continuity. As an LPE interacts with a user, its accumulated experiences shape its memory, identity, behavior, and relationship model.

Two LPEs may begin from the same foundation, but over time they should diverge. Each one becomes shaped by the person, household, work environment, or community it lives with.

That is the commercial opportunity: persistent AI relationships that become more valuable the longer they exist.

Commercial applications

  • Personal AI companions that become unique to each user
  • Education companions that grow alongside a student
  • Professional assistants that accumulate long-term business and workflow context
  • Creator and streamer entities that evolve with their community
  • Family and household AI systems with persistent shared history
  • Future local-first AI interfaces where identity survives device changes, restarts, and upgrades

Current technical foundation

  • APE — Adaptive Persona Engine
  • PSNA — Persistent Structured Neural Archive
  • Burrow — continuity/runtime engine
  • BL Runtime — orchestration/runtime layer
  • Prototype LPE harnesses
